## How to install (3 clicks)

1. Click **Download Lily for Windows** above. The file `Lily-Setup.exe` downloads.
2. Open it (double-click it in your Downloads folder).
3. If a blue box says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   *(Windows shows this for apps from small makers who haven't paid for a certificate. Lily is safe.)*

That's it. Lily appears on your screen, and a **Lily** shortcut is added to your desktop and Start menu.
No admin password is needed.

> Some browsers ask "keep this file?" for new downloads. Choose **Keep**.

## What she does

- Sits where you put her, watches your cursor, grooms, pounces, chases her tail and naps now and then.
- Kneads a little keyboard while you type, gets sleepy if you work for hours, and jumps for joy when you come back from a break.
- **Click** her to pet her. **Drag** her anywhere. **Double-click** or **right-click** her for the menu:
  timer, reminders, focus mode, coat colours, size and settings.

## Remove her

Windows **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Lily → Uninstall**.

## Don't want to install anything?

Download [`Lily-Portable.exe`](https://github.com/vaii7x/lily-desktop-cat/releases/latest/download/Lily-Portable.exe)
instead and double-click it. Nothing is installed; delete the file to remove her.

## Privacy

Lily never connects to the internet and collects nothing. She counts key presses to react to typing but never
records which keys you press. Her settings stay on your PC in `%APPDATA%\Lily`.
